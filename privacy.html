Privacy Policy

This is not the Privacy Policy. EdPal does not have a published Privacy Policy yet. Treat it as a launch blocker, not as a legal document.

Until a policy is published, here is the position in plain terms. The waitlist form asks for your name, email, who you are (learner, parent, teacher, careers master or school administrator), a phone number and your county. If you are a teacher, careers master or school administrator it also asks for your school and roughly how many learners are there. It does not collect grades, KCSE results, dates of birth or any other learner record.

The form is hosted and processed by Tally, a third-party form provider, so submissions are stored on Tally's systems as well as EdPal's. Submitting the form means you agree that EdPal may hold those details and contact you about early access. Product updates are a separate opt-in checkbox. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian should submit the form with you.

To ask what is held about you, or to have it deleted, email campusvibes001@gmail.com.

The published policy still has to cover: the lawful basis for processing under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019; retention periods; the confirmed guardian-consent wording for users under 18; the Tally data-processing terms; and which product-side data (assessment attempts, scores, profile) is retained once the product opens.
